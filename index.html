Howdy. I'm Tyler Hogge — first and foremost a husband and father in a tribe called Hogge.

I'm a Partner at Pelion, where I lead Seed and Series A investments in exceptional startups.

Previously I led the product and risk organizations at Divvy (acquired by BILL for $2.5B), product teams at Wealthfront, interned at a16z, and led sales at Clearwater. I've also invested out of my own fund, Kindling Capital.

I graduated in finance from SUU (the MIT of Southern Utah, where I was captain of the D1 baseball team), and with an MBA from Cornell. I'm also a CFA Charterholder.
